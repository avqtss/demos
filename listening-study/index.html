Study protocol

This listening test evaluates content correctness and perceived spatial accuracy for binaurally decoded AV-QTSS outputs.

Conditions
Eight anonymised processing systems
Planned sample
12-20 adults, maximum 20
Duration
15-30 minutes per participant
Material
Five five-second clips per system
Total
40 formal trials, plus one practice trial
MOS-C
Content correctness, rated from 1 to 5
MOS-S
Perceived spatial accuracy, rated from 1 to 5
Presentation
Randomised and method-blind, using headphones

The system identity and objective metrics are not shown during rating. The exported research record retains the internal condition key and randomisation map for analysis.
