Study protocol

This proposed AV-QTSS task evaluates content correctness and perceived spatial accuracy for binaurally decoded outputs. It is one task within the wider project Listening Studies of Spatial Audio Generation for Audiovisual and Immersive Media.

Conditions
Eight anonymised processing systems
Planned sample
12-20 adults, maximum 20
Duration
15-30 minutes per participant
Material
Five five-second clips per system
Total
40 formal trials, plus one practice trial
MOS-C
Content correctness, rated from 1 to 5
MOS-S
Perceived spatial accuracy, rated from 1 to 5
Presentation
Randomised and method-blind, using headphones
Ethics status
Pending approval; recruitment is closed
Formal submission
Proposed University of Surrey Qualtrics route; not yet configured

The system identity and objective metrics are not shown during rating. The coded research record retains the internal condition key and randomisation map for analysis. Current media are interface-demonstration placeholders, not the final 40 method-specific stimuli.
